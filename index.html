About the Idea

Mini Farm Simulator helps farmers test their farm conditions before planting. The tool gives fast results using clear inputs such as crop type, area, water, soil, salinity, and temperature.

The Farming Problem

Many losses happen because the farmer starts planting without checking the main risk factors first.

🌾

Wrong Crop

Some crops are not suitable for the available water, soil, or temperature.

💧

Water Shortage

Low water directly reduces expected yield and increases the risk level.

🧂

Soil Salinity

High salinity weakens plant growth and lowers the final success rate.

🌡️

High Heat

High temperature affects sensitive crops and reduces production quality.

Mathematical Formulas

The simulator uses fixed and logical formulas. No random values are used.

Simulation Logic

Area in Dunams = If unit is Square Meters: Area / 1000 If unit is Dunams: Area Base Production = Crop Base Yield per Dunam × Area in Dunams Total Water Required = Crop Water Need per Dunam × Area in Dunams Water Factor = Available Water / Total Water Required Maximum value = 1 Minimum value = 0 Temperature Factor = If Average Temperature ≤ Ideal Temperature: Temperature Factor = 1 If Average Temperature > Ideal Temperature: Temperature Difference = Average Temperature - Ideal Temperature Temperature Factor = 1 - (Temperature Difference × 0.02 × Heat Sensitivity) Minimum Temperature Factor = 0.50 Expected Production = Base Production × Water Factor × Soil Factor × Salinity Factor × Fertilizer Factor × Farmer Experience Factor × Temperature Factor Success Rate = Water Factor × Soil Factor × Salinity Factor × Fertilizer Factor × Farmer Experience Factor × Temperature Factor × 100 Risk Level = Low if Success Rate ≥ 75% Medium if Success Rate is from 50% to less than 75% High if Success Rate < 50%
